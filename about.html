About Free Image Compressor

Welcome to Free Image Compressor, a simple online tool designed to help users reduce the file size of their images quickly and easily.

Our goal is to provide a convenient and easy-to-use image compression tool that works directly in your web browser.

What We Offer

You can select an image, adjust the compression quality, compress the image, and download the compressed version.

Our Mission

We want to make useful online tools simple, accessible, and easy to use for everyone.
