Why Convert PNG to JPG?

JPG is widely supported and is often useful when you need a smaller image file for websites, email, social media or other online uses. JPG normally produces smaller files than PNG, although the final size depends on the image.

Important: PNG Transparency

PNG images can contain transparent areas. JPG does not support transparency. When a transparent PNG is converted to JPG, transparent areas are rendered against a solid background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will PNG transparency be preserved after conversion?

No. JPG does not support transparency. Transparent areas in the original PNG will be converted to a solid background.

Does converting PNG to JPG reduce image quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, so some image quality can be lost during conversion. The amount of visible change depends on the original image and compression settings.
