How to Resize an Image Online

1. Select an image from your device.
2. Enter the desired width and height in pixels.

4. Click “Resize Image”.
5. Download your resized image.

The resizing process is performed in your browser using standard web technology. Your image does not need to be uploaded to a separate image-processing server for the resizing operation.
